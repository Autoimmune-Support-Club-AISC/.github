# Autoimmune Support Club (AISC)

**Autoimmune Support Club (AISC)** is a patient-focused community working to make life with autoimmune and chronic illness a little less isolating, confusing, and dismissive.

We started AISC on **September 21, 2024**, founded by **Angel P. and Abdul Aleem Jaseer**.

What began as a small idea has grown considerably since then — into a community, a volunteer network, and a growing body of work focused on **patient experiences, autoimmune awareness, healthcare education, advocacy, and innovation**.

## Why AISC exists

Living with an autoimmune or chronic illness is not only about symptoms, tests, and treatment.

It can also mean being misunderstood, struggling to get a diagnosis, being dismissed by healthcare systems, explaining the same experience repeatedly, or simply not having anyone around who understands what you are going through.

AISC was created around a simple belief:

> **Patients should be heard, taken seriously, and included in the conversations that shape healthcare.**

We want to create spaces where patients can share their experiences, learn from one another, connect with useful resources, and contribute to conversations about how healthcare can improve.

## What we work on

AISC brings together students, patients, volunteers, researchers, designers, writers, developers, and people who simply care about improving patient experiences.

Our work includes:

* **Autoimmune and chronic illness awareness**
* **Patient support and community building**
* **Patient experience and lived-experience projects**
* **Medical gaslighting awareness**
* **Patient-focused healthcare advocacy**
* **Health education and accessible information**
* **Articles, publications, and whitepapers**
* **Webinars and initiatives with healthcare professionals**
* **Student projects focused on healthcare, design, and innovation**
* **Connecting patients and communities with relevant organisations and resources**

We are especially interested in the parts of healthcare that are often discussed less: the human experience behind a diagnosis.

## A growing community

Since September 2024, AISC has continued to grow through the contributions of patients, volunteers, healthcare professionals, students, writers, designers, developers, and supporters from different backgrounds.

Every contribution helps us build something bigger than a support group.

We are building a place where **patient experience can become knowledge, knowledge can become action, and action can lead to better systems.**

## Our approach

AISC is patient-focused, but we do not see patients and healthcare professionals as opposing sides.

We believe better healthcare requires better communication between both.

That means supporting patients while also creating opportunities for doctors, researchers, students, designers, technologists, and advocates to work together on practical problems affecting people with chronic and autoimmune conditions.

Not just another awareness page.
